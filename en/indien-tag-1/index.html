India Day 1

April 10, 2012
Translated by: gpt-4o-2024-08-06 | Original version

Hi friends, it’s 10:00 PM local time. We just got back from dinner to the hotel – and we’re exhausted. The first day was immediately packed with new things. And that’s after a night on the plane with about 1 hour of sleep and a 4:30 time difference in the wrong direction… Here’s the brief photo log: This is near our hotel. Typical: Ultramodern and ancient side by side. Even in the bank, it feels like the 1940s. There is a subway – and it’s really well organized. It must be in a city of 21 million… Everywhere you see beautiful saris. They have temples galore here. On the drive from the airport to the hotel, we saw an elephant on the road. Unfortunately, I wasn’t quick enough with the camera. But there are more animals in the traffic… This is the small replica of the Taj Mahal – doesn’t seem small at all… …and it’s already very impressive. They also have an Arc de Triomphe: They call it “India Gate.” My colleague Christian bravely got “Indian Bread” on the street. As usual here, extremely spicy. Normally the rule is: If you can’t peel it and you can’t cook it – forget it! Another temple: This one is right in the city… In Old Delhi, the streets are even narrower, fuller, and faster. And this is how you look after a day full of new impressions and temples. So, now I have to fall into bed. Good night, – Till.
